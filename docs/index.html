Method, data and caveats

Capacity equation

Volumetric storage capacity follows Goodman et al. (2011), as Eq. 2 of the paper:

M_CO2 = A × h × NTG × φ × (1 − S_wirr) × E × ρ_CO2

where A is the area of the optimal zone, h aquifer thickness, NTG the net-to-gross ratio, φ porosity, Swirr irreducible water saturation, E the storage efficiency factor and ρCO2 in-situ CO₂ density. Each realisation draws every term independently from a truncated normal distribution.

Where the numbers come from

  • Area, porosity, CO₂ density — Table 2 of the paper; porosity and density are the means within each aquifer's optimal zones.
  • Thickness, NTG — Table 2, and the supplementary file 2 Text files/thickness_ntg_averages.txt, derived from petrophysical analysis of twelve wells.
  • Swirr = 0.27 — de Jonge-Anderson et al. (2024a). E = 2 % — Hasbollah et al. (2020).
  • Maps — decoded directly from 9 Optimal zone grids/*.bil, which are GeoTIFFs on a 200 m grid in EPSG:24548. Rendered at native resolution, so pixel areas are exact.

What this tool does not do

  • No caprock integrity, injectivity or permeability assessment — explicitly out of scope in the paper.
  • No dynamic or reservoir simulation; E is a lumped static efficiency factor.
  • Sub-optimal zones are mapped but were not included in the published volumetrics.
  • Correlation between variables (e.g. porosity and thickness) is not modelled; all terms are drawn independently.
